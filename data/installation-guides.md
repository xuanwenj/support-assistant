# Installation Guides & Common Issues

For staff reference when customers ask about installation. These are summaries — full manufacturer instructions are included with the product.

---

## PC-101 / PC-103 — Basin & Bath Mixers

**Installation summary:**
1. Turn off water supply at the mains or isolation valve.
2. Remove old mixer and clean the mounting surface.
3. Fit new flexible hoses (included) to the mixer tails.
4. Secure mixer to basin/bath deck using the supplied fixing kit.
5. Connect hoses to hot and cold supply, turn water back on, check for leaks.

**Common issues:**
- **Leaking at the base after installation** — usually caused by an under-tightened fixing nut, not a faulty unit. Re-tighten before assuming a product fault.
- **Low flow after installation** — check that the aerator (the small mesh disc in the spout tip) isn't blocked with plumber's tape residue or debris from new pipework.
- Customer-supplied units: if the customer purchased the mixer elsewhere and is asking us to install it, confirm it meets the minimum pressure rating for their system before booking installation (see product-catalog).

## SH-201 — Rainfall Overhead Shower Set

**Installation summary:**
1. Requires a shower arm outlet at approximately 2100mm height (standard).
2. Wall-mount the shower arm bracket, ensuring it's fixed into a solid framing member, not just the wall lining.
3. Connect the shower head to the arm — hand-tighten plus a quarter turn with a wrench, do not overtighten (can crack the head fitting).
4. Test flow and check the wall fixing point for movement.

**Common issues:**
- Below 100 kPa incoming pressure, customers may find the flow "weak" even though the unit is working correctly — this is expected behaviour at low pressure, not a fault. The pressure-limiting valve (PC-111) only helps if pressure is *too high*; it does not help with low pressure.
- If the wall lining is not backed by solid framing, a support plate must be used — flag this to customers with older villas/bungalows before they attempt DIY installation.

## SH-203 — Twin Shower System

**Installation summary:**
1. Requires the PC-110 diverter valve to be installed in-wall before wall lining is closed up — **this must be planned before a renovation reaches the wall-lining stage**.
2. Overhead and handheld outlets are plumbed from the diverter, not directly from the mains line.
3. Pressure-test the diverter before closing the wall.

**Common issues:**
- The most frequent support call for this product is customers who didn't order the diverter valve in time and have already closed up the wall — this requires opening the wall again. Always confirm PC-110 is on the order before a twin shower purchase is finalised.
- Minimum 200 kPa is required for both outlets to run acceptably; below this, running both together will noticeably reduce pressure at each outlet.

## TL-302 — Wall-Faced Toilet Suite (In-Wall Cistern)

**Installation summary:**
1. The in-wall cistern frame (WF-401) must be installed and pressure-tested **before** wall lining, tiling, or plastering begins.
2. Frame is fixed to floor and structural framing — not a lightweight fixing.
3. Pan is fitted to the frame after wall finishing is complete, using the frame's adjustable pan connector to align with the finished wall surface.

**Common issues:**
- Similar to SH-203: the biggest source of install failures is not ordering/installing the frame early enough in a renovation timeline. Recommend customers confirm their builder has the frame on-site before wall closure.
- Flush plate not sitting flush against the wall — usually caused by the wall lining thickness not matching the frame's adjustable depth range (adjustable 100–160mm); check finished wall thickness before installation.

## General Notes for Staff

- Any installation involving altering existing pipework (not just swapping a fitting) legally requires a registered plumber in most cases — if a customer describes this scope, recommend they engage a licensed plumber rather than DIY, and note this is for compliance reasons, not a sales upsell.
- If unsure whether a specific customer scenario is DIY-appropriate, escalate to the in-store technical advisor rather than guessing.
