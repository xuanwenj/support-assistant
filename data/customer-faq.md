# Customer FAQ

Collected from common customer questions at the counter and over the phone. These are written in customers' own words, not official terminology — use this to test whether retrieval can connect casual phrasing to the formal product/policy documents.

---

**Q: My new tap is dripping from underneath, is that covered?**
A: A drip at the base right after fitting is almost always a loose fixing nut, not a fault — see the installation notes for basin and bath mixers. If it keeps dripping after re-tightening, it may be a genuine defect and would fall under warranty, but only if the unit wasn't self-installed by the customer for products that require professional fitting (check the returns and warranty policy for which products that applies to).

**Q: Can I bring back a toilet I bought if I changed my mind and haven't opened the box?**
A: Change-of-mind returns are accepted for unopened, unused items within the standard return window. Wall-faced toilet suites with in-wall cisterns are a special case because they're often ordered alongside a wall frame — check whether the frame was part of a bundle purchase before processing, since bundle items may have different return terms.

**Q: I've got an old house with a header tank in the roof, will your rainfall shower head even work?**
A: That's a low-pressure or gravity-fed system. Not every shower head is rated for that — the basin mixer and the compact shower models are safer picks for gravity-fed setups. If you're not sure what pressure the customer has, don't guess — recommend they get a plumber to test it, or point them to the pressure-limiting valve if their reading is on the high side.

**Q: Do trade customers get a discount and how do they sign up?**
A: Yes, there's a separate trade price for account holders. If someone asks about trade pricing but doesn't have an account yet, don't quote them the trade number — direct them to set up an account first.

**Q: The shower diverter I bought is making a banging noise when I turn it off.**
A: That's usually water hammer, not a fault with the diverter itself — it happens when a valve closes quickly and the water column stops abruptly. There's an inline accessory designed specifically to fix this; it's a cheap add-on, not a replacement product.

**Q: I bought the twin shower bundle but only want to return the handheld part, is that possible?**
A: Bundle pricing only applies when everything is bought together in one transaction — partial returns from a bundle need to be handled carefully because returning one component may affect the discount that applied to the rest. Flag this to a supervisor rather than processing it as a standard single-item return.

**Q: Is there a warranty on the pressure valve or is that just an accessory?**
A: Accessories still carry their own warranty terms even though they're not the "main" product — don't assume a cheap add-on has no warranty just because of its price point.

**Q: What's the difference between a wall-mounted and a close-coupled toilet, and does it change installation cost?**
A: Wall-faced units need a wall frame installed before the wall lining goes up — that's a bigger job than a close-coupled unit which just bolts to the floor. If a customer is renovating rather than doing new construction, that timing detail matters a lot and is worth flagging even if they didn't ask about installation directly.

**Q: Someone told me mains pressure and town supply pressure are the same thing — is that right?**
A: In casual conversation, yes, people use those terms interchangeably to mean the same pressure range. Staff should recognize both phrasings refer to the same thing when a customer describes their setup.

**Q: Can I get parts for a product you don't sell anymore?**
A: Not something covered in our current documentation — this should be escalated rather than guessed at, since giving a wrong answer about discontinued parts could send a customer down the wrong path entirely.
